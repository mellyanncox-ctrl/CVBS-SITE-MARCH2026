Brisbane overview

Why book your conference in Brisbane

CVBS has been sourcing Brisbane conference venues since 1989. Brisbane's compact CBD means most delegates can walk between the convention centre, hotels, and dining precincts — a major operational advantage. With the 2032 Olympics driving significant infrastructure investment, the city's already strong hotel and event venue stock continues to grow.

Common questions

Brisbane venue FAQs

How far in advance should I book a Brisbane venue?
For 100+ delegates, 9–12 months ahead. Brisbane's hotel stock is tightening with Olympic infrastructure investment and growing demand. For smaller groups under 50, 3–6 months is usually sufficient.
What does a Brisbane conference venue cost per delegate?
Day delegate rates (DDR) typically range from $90–$160 per person per day including room hire, AV, and catering. Residential packages range from $255–$450+ per person. Brisbane generally offers better value than Sydney or Melbourne for equivalent quality.
Is Brisbane or Gold Coast better for a conference?
Brisbane wins for domestically-focused corporate conferences where a professional CBD setting matters. Gold Coast wins for groups who want to combine the conference with leisure activities and a resort atmosphere. We service both and can help you decide based on your group's priorities.
Can you beat the rate I'd get direct?
In most cases, yes. Our collective volume gives us negotiating leverage that individual bookers rarely achieve. Clients consistently confirm our rates match or beat going direct.
Do you cover venues outside the Brisbane CBD?
Yes — all of Greater Brisbane including the Sunshine Coast (45 mins north), Gold Coast (1hr south), and the Scenic Rim. If you're open to regional options, tell us in your brief.
