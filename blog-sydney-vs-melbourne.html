Sydney vs Melbourne for your annual conference: an honest comparison

Karen Jepson, CVBS
6 min read
March 2026

It's the question we get every time a client has flexibility on location: Sydney or Melbourne? Both cities have excellent conference infrastructure, strong hotel supply, and direct international flights. The choice usually comes down to what you need from the destination, and how much flexibility you have on timing and budget.

After placing hundreds of events in both cities over 35 years, here's our honest comparison.

"Melbourne wins on variety. Sydney wins on views. The brief tells us which one actually matters for your event."

Venue supply and type

Both cities have abundant conference venue supply, but the character is quite different.

Sydney

Sydney's conference market is dominated by large hotel conference centres in the CBD and Darling Harbour. The ICC Sydney is Australia's largest convention centre, located on the waterfront. Sydney has excellent options for large conferences (200–1,000 delegates) and strong supply at the 150–300 delegate range in Darling Harbour hotels. Boutique and unique venues exist but are comparatively harder to source.

Melbourne

Melbourne has more diverse venue supply. The CBD has strong hotel conference product, but Melbourne is also known for its converted warehouse spaces, laneway venues, rooftop terraces and cultural institutions. For events where the venue needs to make a statement, product launches, association conferences, awards nights, Melbourne's venue variety is harder to beat.

Pricing

In general, Sydney runs 5–15% more expensive than Melbourne at comparable venue tiers, largely because of Darling Harbour's premium positioning and the high demand at Sydney's waterfront hotels.

That said, competitive pricing exists in both cities, particularly if you have date flexibility. Melbourne's shoulder season (April and September) often produces excellent value at 4 and 5-star properties that are keen to fill inventory.

Delegate experience

If your delegates are flying in from interstate or internationally, both cities offer strong options, but the experience differs.

Sydney delivers the iconic Australian city experience. Delegates from overseas consistently rate Sydney highly. Pre and post-conference activities are strong: the harbour, Bondi, the Opera House. The flip side is that Sydney's transport can be frustrating, and venue transfers between the CBD and Darling Harbour eat into your program.

Melbourne is compact in a way Sydney isn't. Most conference hotel properties are within walking distance of excellent restaurants, bars and cultural institutions. The laneway culture and food scene are genuine differentiators for evening social programs. Delegates who've done Sydney before often respond very positively to Melbourne.

Seasonality

This is where the choice often gets made.

  • If your event is June–August: Sydney is generally preferred, Melbourne winters are genuinely cold and wet, which affects outdoor social programs and delegate mood. Sydney's winters are mild.
  • If your event is October–March: Melbourne becomes a stronger choice. Its spring and summer weather is excellent for outdoor events, and the city comes alive. Sydney in February–March can be uncomfortably hot and humid.
  • April–May: Both cities are excellent. This is the sweet spot for conferences in either location.

Flight access

Both cities have major international airports with frequent domestic connections. For government and association conferences drawing delegates from across Australia, Melbourne's central position gives it a slight edge. Canberra delegates often prefer Melbourne over Sydney for domestic connectivity.

Our recommendation

There is no universal answer, but here's how we think about it:

Choose Sydney if: Your delegates include international attendees for whom the Sydney experience is meaningful, you need 300+ delegate capacity with harbour views, or your event is June–August.

Choose Melbourne if: The social program and destination experience is important, your event involves a product launch or awards night where venue character matters, you need boutique or heritage spaces, or your event is October–March.

When clients come to us with genuine flexibility on city, we look at your brief in full, delegate profile, event purpose, dates, budget and program, and tell you where we can get you the best result. More often than not, one city clearly wins on the brief's specific requirements.
