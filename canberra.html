Canberra overview

Why book your conference in Canberra

CVBS has been sourcing Canberra conference venues since 1989. Canberra is Australia's most underrated conference city. The compact geography means delegates can move between venues, government departments, and cultural institutions easily — and the hotel and hospitality scene has improved markedly over the past decade. For government, association, and policy-focused events, Canberra is often the natural and most practical choice.

Common questions

Canberra venue FAQs

How far in advance should I book a Canberra venue?
For 100+ delegates, 6–9 months ahead. Canberra's venue supply is more limited than Sydney or Melbourne, and parliamentary sitting periods create significant demand peaks (typically February–June and August–November). For smaller groups, 3–4 months is usually workable.
What does a Canberra conference venue cost per delegate?
Day delegate rates (DDR) typically range from $85–$155 per person per day, including room hire, AV, and catering. Residential packages range from $240–$420+ per person. Canberra generally offers good value compared to Sydney or Melbourne.
Is Canberra only suitable for government events?
Definitely not. Canberra suits any organisation with national membership or reach — associations, peak bodies, professional organisations, and any group that benefits from proximity to government decision-makers. The city's national institutions also make outstanding gala dinner venues for any sector.
Can you beat the rate I'd get direct?
In most cases, yes. Our collective volume gives us meaningful leverage with Canberra's hotel groups. Clients consistently confirm our rates match or beat going direct.
Do you cover venues outside the Canberra CBD?
Yes — including the Southern Tablelands, Snowy Mountains, and the ACT's wider region. If you're open to combining Canberra with a day's offsite in the surrounding region, tell us in your brief.
