From Surfers Paradise's iconic beachfront strip to the lush hinterland, the Gold Coast offers remarkably diverse venue options within a compact geography.

Surfers Paradise
The iconic highrise strip. Central, walkable, buzzing. Excellent for delegates wanting entertainment on their doorstep after sessions.

Common questions

Gold Coast venue FAQs

How far in advance should I book a Gold Coast venue?
For 100+ delegates, 9–12 months ahead. The Gold Coast is heavily booked during school holidays, Commonwealth Games legacy periods, and Q1/Q4. For smaller groups, 3–6 months is usually sufficient. We regularly find good last-minute options — submit a brief and we'll assess availability immediately.
What does a Gold Coast conference venue cost per delegate?
Day delegate rates (DDR) typically range from $90–$165 per person per day, including room hire, AV, and catering. Residential packages range from $260–$480+ per person. Beachfront properties carry a premium; hinterland venues tend to offer better value per dollar.
Is the Gold Coast better than Brisbane for a conference?
It depends on your group. The Gold Coast wins on atmosphere, leisure activities, and delegate engagement — people are more energised at the beach. Brisbane is better for domestically-focused corporate events where flying time is a concern. We service both and can help you weigh up the tradeoffs for your specific brief.
Can you beat the rate I'd get direct?
In most cases, yes. Our collective volume gives us negotiating leverage that individual bookers rarely achieve. Clients consistently confirm our rates match or beat going direct — and we handle all the negotiation and administration at no cost to you.
Do you cover venues outside the main Gold Coast strip?
Yes — all of Greater Gold Coast including Sanctuary Cove, Coomera, Tamborine Mountain, and the broader hinterland. If you're open to combining beach and hinterland elements across multiple days, tell us in your brief and we'll include split-location options.
