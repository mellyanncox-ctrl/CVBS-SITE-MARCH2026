Common questions

Melbourne venue FAQs

How far in advance should I book a Melbourne venue?
For 100+ delegates, 9–18 months ahead. Melbourne is Australia's most in-demand conference city and books out quickly, particularly in March–April (F1, AFL start) and September–October (AFL Finals, MIGA). For smaller groups under 30, 3–6 months is usually workable.
What does a Melbourne conference venue cost per delegate?
Day delegate rates (DDR) typically range from $100–$185 per person per day, including room hire, AV, and catering. Residential packages range from $295–$520+ per person. Melbourne's pricing is broadly comparable to Sydney, with similar depth of supply at higher price points.
Melbourne or Sydney — which is better for a conference?
Both are exceptional. Melbourne wins on dining and cultural experience, unique venue variety, and delegate satisfaction scores. Sydney wins on harbour views and international delegate appeal. We service both and can walk you through the tradeoffs for your specific group and agenda.
Can you beat the rate I'd get direct?
In most cases, yes. Our collective Melbourne volume gives us meaningful leverage with the major hotel groups. Clients consistently confirm our rates match or beat going direct.
Do you cover venues outside the Melbourne CBD?
Yes — all of Greater Melbourne including the Yarra Valley, Mornington Peninsula, Phillip Island, Geelong, and the Surf Coast. Tell us if you're open to regional options and we'll include them in your shortlist.
