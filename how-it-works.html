Step by step

How we find your perfect venue

From the moment you submit a brief to the moment your event concludes.

1
Step one
You submit your brief
Tell us about your event using our online brief form. It takes around five minutes. We ask about event type, location, dates, delegate numbers, budget range, accommodation needs and any specific requirements.
What we ask for:
Event type, location and approximate dates
Number of delegates and accommodation requirements
Budget per head or total event budget
AV requirements, room setup, catering and any deal-breakers
Takes around 5 minutes
2
Step two
Karen or Anthony reviews your brief
Your brief lands directly with Karen or Anthony, not a junior coordinator, not a system. They read it personally, assess your requirements and begin shortlisting. If they have questions, they'll reach out the within the day.
What happens behind the scenes:
Brief reviewed personally by Karen or Anthony
Suitable venues identified from 35 years of direct relationships
Venues contacted directly for availability and rates
Rates and inclusions negotiated on your behalf
Within a few hours of receiving your brief
3
Step three
You receive your shortlist
Within 48 hours, you receive a fully costed shortlist, presented side by side so you can compare clearly. Each option includes room hire, AV, catering, accommodation rates and any negotiated inclusions.
Within 48 hours, guaranteed
4
Step four
You choose your venue
Review the options and let us know your preference. We'll arrange site inspections if needed. There's no pressure and no obligation until you're ready to proceed.
At your own pace, no pressure
5
Step five
We handle contracting and coordination
Once you've chosen, we manage the contracting process, checking terms, inclusions and conditions. We remain your point of contact through to your event, and if anything goes wrong on the day, we're the ones who fix it.
Through to event completion, then your venue pays us

Common questions

Things people wonder about

"If it's free, what's the catch?"
No catch. At the completion of your event, the venue pays us a brokerage fee. This fee does not come from you, and rates are never inflated to cover it. Clients consistently tell us our negotiated rates match or beat what they'd get going direct, because of the volume of business we bring to venues.
"Are your recommendations biased toward certain venues?"
No. CVBS is completely independent, not aligned with any hotel chain, venue group or booking network. We have no preferred supplier agreements. Every venue we suggest is based solely on what is right for your event.
"Can you actually beat the rate I'd get direct?"
In most cases, yes. One client price-checked CVBS every year for nine consecutive years. Every single time, she could not have got a cheaper rate going direct to the venue. Our volume gives us negotiating leverage individual bookers don't have.
"What if I only have a rough idea of what I need?"
That's fine, and quite common. Tell us what you do know. We'll ask the right questions to fill in the gaps and often suggest options you wouldn't have considered.
"How quickly do you actually respond?"
We guarantee a response within 48 hours for every brief. Karen or Anthony personally handles every brief. You won't receive an automated acknowledgement and nothing else.
"Do you only cover Sydney and major cities?"
No, we cover all of Australia and many international destinations including Hunter Valley, Blue Mountains, Yarra Valley, Fiji, Bali, New Zealand and Singapore.
