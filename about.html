How it started

A business built on trust, not technology

CVBS was founded in 1989 with a simple conviction: that finding the right conference venue should be personal. That someone who truly understands your event will always find a better venue than a search engine. Karen joined in 1999 as Business Development Manager, opening the Brisbane division and building the client and venue relationships that would come to define the business.

In 2002, Karen acquired the Brisbane division, and in 2007 she and Anthony took ownership of the whole company. For nearly three decades between them, they have been the people clients call when an event matters.

Together, they have sourced venues for events from four-person board meetings to 300-delegate national conferences, for government departments, ASX-listed companies, pharmaceutical firms and professional associations.

How we work

What makes CVBS different

01
Personal from the start
Every brief is handled by Karen or Anthony directly. You speak to the people who know the venues.
02
We inspect venues ourselves
Karen and Anthony regularly travel to inspect properties. When we recommend a venue, it's from direct experience, not a brochure.
03
We negotiate on your behalf
We don't just find options, we negotiate rates, inclusions and terms. Clients consistently get better rates through CVBS than going direct.
04
We stay with you
From brief to booking to event day. We remain your point of contact. If something goes wrong at the venue, we're the ones who fix it.
05
We're completely unbiased
CVBS is not aligned with any hotel group or venue chain. We recommend what's right for you, not what earns us the highest commission.
06
We're free to you
Our service costs you nothing. We receive a brokerage fee from the venue at event completion. Rates are never inflated to cover it.
