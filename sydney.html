Common questions

Sydney venue FAQs

How far in advance should I book?
For 50+ delegates, 6–12 months ahead. Sydney fills quickly in Q1 and Q4. That said, we regularly find excellent last-minute options, submit your brief and we'll assess availability.
What does a Sydney venue cost per delegate?
Day delegate rates (DDR) range from $95–$180 per person per day, covering room hire, AV, catering. Residential packages range from $280–$500+ per person.
Can you beat the rate I'd get direct?
In most cases, yes. Our collective volume gives us negotiating leverage that individual bookers rarely achieve. Clients consistently confirm our rates match or beat going direct.
Do you cover venues outside the CBD?
Yes, all of Greater Sydney, Blue Mountains, Hunter Valley, Southern Highlands and beyond. If you're open to travel, tell us and we'll include regional options in your shortlist.
