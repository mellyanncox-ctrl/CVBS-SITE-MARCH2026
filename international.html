Common questions

International FAQs

Do you charge more for international venue finding?+
No. Our service is completely free to you regardless of destination. We are paid a brokerage fee by the venue after your event concludes — the same model as our Australian service.
How far in advance should I book an international conference?+
For popular destinations like Fiji and Bali, 6–12 months ahead is recommended for peak season. Singapore and Bangkok have more venue supply and can often be sourced with 3–6 months lead time. Smaller island buyout properties can book out 12–18 months in advance for peak periods.
Can you arrange group flights and transfers?+
CVBS specialises in venue finding and negotiation. We can recommend trusted travel partners for group flights, but this sits outside our core service. For transfers within the destination, we coordinate with the venue and can include this in the brief.
What information do I need to submit an international brief?+
The same information as any brief: destination preference, approximate dates, delegate numbers, number of nights, budget per person (or total), and any specific requirements. If you are open on destination, tell us your delegate profile and budget and we will recommend the best options.
